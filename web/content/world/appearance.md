# What things look like in Aetheria
- The Corporate Consortium's emblem is a silver circle cut by a single vertical line, and it is displayed on its towers in the Crown District.
- The Council's seal is a black disc with seven small white points around its edge, one for each member.
- Security force officers wear dark grey coats with a pale blue band on the left sleeve.
- Security drones are small, dark and quiet, and each one carries a pale blue light underneath.
- The Glassline trains are long silver cars with tall windows, and their lights turn amber late at night.
- The Rotunda is a round hall of dark stone and glass at the centre of the Crown District, with a dome that glows white at night.
- Neon signs in the lower districts are mostly teal, amber and red, and they are reflected in the wet streets.
- The Meridian Exchange is a tall grey block ringed by cooling towers, and its upper floors glow faintly blue at night.
- The District Nine night market is built under corrugated metal roofs strung with lamps.
- The Halcyon Terraces are lined with pale stone houses and trimmed hedges, and they look out over the city walls.
- Rain in Aetheria usually falls straight down and turns the neon signs into long streaks of colour.
