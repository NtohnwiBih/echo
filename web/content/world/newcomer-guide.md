# A visitor's guide to Aetheria
- A visitor to Aetheria needs a district pass to cross from one district to another, and a visitor without a comms device is given a paper pass at the first checkpoint.
- Checkpoints are staffed by the security forces, who ask each visitor for a pass and a reason for the visit.
- The Glassline is the easiest way to cross the city at night, and the last trains leave late, after which only the all-night cafés stay open.
- The all-night cafés along the Glassline are where visitors and residents mix, and they are the easiest place to meet people.
- The District Nine night market is the liveliest place in the lower districts after dark, with food stalls, repair stalls and card games.
- The Halcyon Terraces are the most scenic district, with views over the city walls, and they are quiet and expensive.
- The Crown District is lined with corporate towers and is mostly quiet after the offices close.
- Tidewater is the place to see the waterfront and the loading yards, and its streets flood in storms.
- Cinder Row is crowded and built around old furnaces, and residents advise visitors to go there with a local.
- Rain is common in Aetheria and turns without warning, so residents advise visitors to carry a coat.
- Many residents avoid talking about the Council or Ilan Vorr in public, and visitors are advised not to take it personally.
