# The Corporate Consortium
- The Corporate Consortium operates the Meridian Grid under a contract from the Council.
- The Consortium's executives call the Grid the heartbeat of the city.
- The Consortium says the recent blackouts in the lower districts are routine maintenance.
- The Consortium's towers stand in the Crown District.
- Aetheria's security network runs on Grid power, and the Consortium controls that power.
- The names of the Consortium's directors are not published.
- The Consortium has declined to comment on Ilan Vorr.
