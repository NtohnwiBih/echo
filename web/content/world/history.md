# History: the Nine Days
- About fourteen years ago, a failure now called the Nine Days cut power to the whole of Aetheria for nine days.
- The number of people who died during the Nine Days has never been published.
- After the Nine Days, the Council sealed the districts and set up the checkpoint system.
- The Council said the sealing would be temporary, and it has never been lifted.
- The Meridian Grid was built after the Nine Days to give the city a reliable power supply.
- The Council gave the Corporate Consortium the contract to operate the Grid once it was built.
- The Council's powers grew during the Nine Days, and the emergency measures it adopted then have never been withdrawn.
