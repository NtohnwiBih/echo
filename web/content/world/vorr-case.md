# The disappearance of Ilan Vorr (public knowledge)
- Ilan Vorr, a senior city engineer, vanished eleven days ago.
- Vorr led the Meridian Grid project.
- The official account says Vorr left the city of his own accord, and the Council calls his disappearance a private matter.
- Vorr's access badge was last scanned at the control hall inside the Meridian Exchange on the night he vanished, at a time that has not been released.
- There is no exit scan for Vorr's badge from the control hall.
- The control hall's camera footage from the night Vorr vanished has not been released.
- No witness has come forward.
- No checkpoint record of Vorr leaving the city has been made public.
- No suspect has been named.
- No farewell message from Vorr has been made public.
- It is not publicly known who last saw Vorr.
- It is not publicly known where Vorr lived.
- It is not publicly known who Vorr's closest colleagues were, or whether he has family.
- It is not publicly known what Vorr worked on in his last week.
- No threats against Vorr have been made public, and it is not publicly known whether he was afraid of anyone.
- It is not publicly known whether Vorr's comms device has been found.
