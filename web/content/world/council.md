# The Council
- The Council governs Aetheria and has seven members.
- The Council has no single leader.
- The chair of the Council rotates every year, and the current chair is not publicly named.
- The names of the seven Council members are not published.
- The Council meets in the Rotunda in the Crown District.
- The Council controls the district passes, the security forces and the release of official records.
- The Council gave the Corporate Consortium the contract to operate the Meridian Grid.
- Council members choose their own successors.
- The Council has said nothing about Ilan Vorr beyond calling his disappearance a private matter.
