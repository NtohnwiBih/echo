# The six districts
- The Crown District is an upper district of corporate towers and Council chambers, and the Council meets there in the Rotunda.
- People in the lower districts say that decisions made in the Crown District rarely reach the streets unchanged.
- The Internal Affairs Division has its offices in the Crown District.
- The Halcyon Terraces are the wealthy residential district of the upper city, built in terraces above the city walls.
- The Foundry Ward is the middle district, full of workshops, factories and small offices, and most of the independent printers in Aetheria work there.
- The Meridian Exchange stands in the Foundry Ward.
- Tidewater is a lower district on the waterfront, where cargo is loaded in the loading yards and the streets flood in storms.
- Cinder Row is a crowded lower district built around old furnaces that no longer run.
- District Nine is a lower district of night markets, unregistered workshops and cameras that fail without explanation.
- Kael patrolled District Nine when he served in the security forces.
- Every district has checkpoints, and the security forces staff them.
