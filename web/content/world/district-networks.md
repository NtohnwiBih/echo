# The district networks
- The district networks are informal groups of residents, fixers and reporters who trade information the towers will not release.
- The district networks operate in every lower district and meet in night markets and cafés.
- Trust in the networks is built through favours, and a person who passes on false information is cut off.
- Information that travels through the networks is usually unconfirmed.
