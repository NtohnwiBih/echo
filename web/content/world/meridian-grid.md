# The Meridian Grid
- The Meridian Grid is the power network that supplies Aetheria's trains, towers and security network.
- The lower districts depend on the Grid for all of their power.
- Senior city engineer Ilan Vorr led the Meridian Grid project.
- The Corporate Consortium operates the Grid.
- The Grid is monitored and controlled from the control hall inside the Meridian Exchange.
- Entry to the control hall requires an access badge, and every badge scan is logged on the way in and on the way out.
- Rolling blackouts have struck the lower districts in recent weeks.
- It is not publicly known who directs the Grid's daily operation inside the Consortium.
- Whether the Grid is safe has not been publicly assessed.
- The technical design of the Meridian Grid has not been published.
