# Aetheria: overview
- Aetheria is a walled city of sealed districts, run by the Council.
- Aetheria has six districts: the Crown District, the Halcyon Terraces, the Foundry Ward, Tidewater, Cinder Row and District Nine.
- Movement between the districts is controlled by checkpoints, and a citizen needs a district pass to cross from one district to another.
- Most citizens carry a personal comms device that also serves as their district pass.
- The Crown District and the Halcyon Terraces are the upper districts, the Foundry Ward lies between them and the lower districts, and Tidewater, Cinder Row and District Nine are the lower districts.
- Neon signs light the streets of Aetheria, and rain is common.
- The lower districts flood in storms.
- The towers of the upper city belong to corporations, and the Council governs from among them.
- The Meridian Grid powers Aetheria's trains, towers and security network.
