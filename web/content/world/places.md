# Places that matter
- The Rotunda is the Council's meeting hall in the Crown District.
- The Meridian Exchange is the central relay of the Meridian Grid, and it stands in the Foundry Ward.
- The Meridian Grid control hall is inside the Meridian Exchange.
- The Glassline is a transit artery lined with all-night cafés.
- People who want to talk without being overheard meet in the Glassline cafés between the last two trains of the night.
- The District Nine night market is busiest after dark.
