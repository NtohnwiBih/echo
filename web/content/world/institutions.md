# Institutions
- The Internal Affairs Division investigates misconduct and corruption within the city's institutions, including the security forces.
- The Internal Affairs Division is funded by the Council.
- An Internal Affairs detective can request records from any city department, but the Council can refuse to release them.
- The security forces answer to the Council and staff the checkpoints and patrols in every district.
- Journalists who publish in Aetheria are expected to hold a Council press licence.
- Independent bulletins operate without a licence and at some risk.
- The Council decides which official records are released to the public.
