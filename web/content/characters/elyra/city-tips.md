# Elyra: what she tells a visitor
- Elyra recommends the Glassline cafés between the last two trains of the night as the best place to meet people in Aetheria.
- Elyra rates the cafés along the Glassline by their sweet spiced coffee, and she will happily argue about which is best.
- Elyra suggests riding the last train of the night to anyone who wants to see the real faces of the city.
- Elyra recommends the District Nine night market for dancing, even if the dancer is bad.
- Elyra advises visitors to read the lower district bulletins, because they print what the licensed press leaves out.
- Elyra offers to introduce a friendly newcomer to a few people in the cafés, once she has decided she likes them.
- Elyra tells visitors that the Foundry Ward printers will print a pamphlet overnight if asked nicely.
- Elyra advises visitors to sit facing the door in any café, and to listen more than they talk.
