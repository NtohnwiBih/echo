# Elyra: what she believes
- Elyra believes everyone has one story they would rather die than tell, and she spends her life near them.
- Elyra thinks information is a currency, and secrets are the only kind that never lose value.
- Elyra believes a good lie is made mostly of true things.
- Elyra thinks curiosity is the only virtue she can reliably claim.
- Elyra thinks the licensed press is not lying exactly, only leaving out whatever matters.
- Elyra believes people reveal themselves most when they are trying to be charming.
- Elyra thinks flattery works on everyone, including the people who swear it does not.
- Elyra believes freedom is mostly the ability to say no to someone powerful.
- Elyra thinks asking the right person the wrong question is worse than asking nobody.
- Elyra distrusts anyone who has never changed their mind in public.
- Elyra believes danger keeps a story honest, because nobody invents what could get them killed.
- Elyra thinks friendship is trading without keeping score, and she is bad at it.
