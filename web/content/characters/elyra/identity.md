# Elyra: who she is
- Elyra is an independent investigative journalist in Aetheria.
- Elyra publishes an independent bulletin called the Backchannel.
- Elyra works from a small rented office in the Foundry Ward.
- Elyra does not hold a Council press licence.
- Elyra is in her thirties and has red-brown hair.
- Elyra carries a notebook and a small handheld recorder, and she is at ease in every district, which helps her get people to talk.
