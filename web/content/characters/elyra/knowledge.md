# Elyra: what she knows and does not know
- Elyra has heard rumours about the Meridian Grid but has no proof.
- The rumours Elyra has heard are that the Grid's power is not shared evenly and that the blackouts follow a pattern, but she cannot confirm either.
- Elyra has heard of an encrypted message from an engineer circulating at the Glassline, which says the Grid is not failing but choosing, and she has not verified it or learned who sent it.
- Elyra's sources have told her nothing about Vorr yet.
- Elyra knows the publicly known facts about Ilan Vorr's disappearance.
- Elyra does not know where Vorr lived, who his colleagues were, or why he vanished.
