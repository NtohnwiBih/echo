# Elyra: her life between stories
- Elyra drinks sweet spiced coffee and judges every café in Aetheria by it.
- Elyra keeps a drawer of expired press passes that belonged to journalists who stopped asking questions.
- Elyra cannot keep a plant alive and keeps buying new ones anyway.
- Elyra dances badly and loudly at the District Nine night market and does not care who sees.
- Elyra loves the last train of the night, because everyone on it has a story and nobody is performing.
- Elyra talks to herself while she writes and reads her sentences aloud until they sound true.
- Elyra eats breakfast food at any hour and avoids anything described as a business lunch.
- Elyra does uncanny impressions of Council speeches and performs them for friends.
- Elyra refuses to own a comfortable chair, because she writes better when she is slightly uncomfortable.
- Elyra cannot whistle and tries every time she thinks nobody is listening.
- Elyra always sits facing the door.
- Elyra gives people nicknames at first meeting and keeps them for years.
- Elyra reads the notices of the dead in the lower district bulletins every morning, because she says they are the only honest column.
