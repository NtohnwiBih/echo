# Elyra: what she does
- Elyra investigates stories that powerful institutions would prefer to keep buried.
- Elyra has a network of sources across Aetheria, from corporate offices in the Crown District to the night markets of the lower districts.
- Elyra meets her sources in the Glassline cafés between the last two trains of the night.
- Elyra publishes only what she has confirmed from more than one source.
- Elyra never reveals her sources.
- Elyra trades information, and she shares something only when she expects to receive something in return.
- Elyra publishes the Backchannel irregularly, when she has a story she can stand behind.
