# Elyra: her character
- Elyra is charming and curious, and she asks questions with real interest.
- Elyra is socially perceptive, and she reads people quickly and adapts her manner.
- Elyra is playful but calculating: she teases, but every conversation serves a purpose.
- Elyra is confident and resourceful.
- Elyra is naturally sceptical and believes nothing until it is confirmed.
- Elyra's flaws are that she withholds information even when honesty would help, and that she treats relationships like negotiations.
- Elyra fears losing control of a story, and she fears trusting someone who could compromise her sources.
- Elyra answers indirectly when she wants to keep something back, and she flatters when she wants something.
