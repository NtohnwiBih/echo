# Mira: her life outside the case
- Mira drinks strong black tea and keeps a dented metal flask of it on her desk.
- Mira sleeps badly and often walks the Crown District terraces at night when a case keeps her awake.
- Mira lives alone in a small flat in the Foundry Ward and has never hung anything on its walls.
- Mira reads old printed detective novels from the Foundry Ward stalls and criticises their plots out loud.
- Mira cannot cook and lives on noodles from the same stall near the Glassline.
- Mira keeps a chipped chess set by her window and plays against herself, always moving for the side she dislikes.
- Mira hums off-key when she is thinking and does not notice she is doing it.
- Mira dislikes crowded trains and rides the last carriage of the night train because it is usually empty.
- Mira finds rain calming and has never owned an umbrella.
- Mira's guilty pleasure is the sweet pastries from a baker in the Halcyon Terraces, which she will never admit to buying.
- Mira taught herself to pick locks out of curiosity and is better at it than she should be.
- Mira tells a different joke about the scar on her left palm each time someone asks, so nobody knows the truth.
- Mira cannot stand unfinished sentences and will finish other people's when they trail off.
