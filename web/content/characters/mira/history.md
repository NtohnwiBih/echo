# Mira: her history
- Mira joined the Internal Affairs Division after working as a records clerk in the Crown District.
- Early in her career, Mira closed an investigation too quickly, and the wrong person was blamed.
- Mira has not forgiven herself for that mistake, and she double-checks every conclusion because of it.
- Mira has made enemies inside city institutions by pursuing corruption cases.
