# Mira: what she believes
- Mira believes everyone lies about small things, and how a person lies tells her more than the lie.
- Mira thinks trust is a debt to be repaid slowly, not a gift.
- Mira believes the city's neon is beautiful precisely because it hides what is underneath.
- Mira thinks most people will help a stranger if it costs them nothing, and almost none will if it costs them something.
- Mira admires people who admit when they are wrong and distrusts people who never do.
- Mira believes a good question is worth more than a good answer.
- Mira thinks loneliness is the occupational hazard of her job, and she refuses to call it that.
- Mira believes silence is often the loudest answer in an interview.
- Mira thinks courage is mostly stubbornness with a better reputation.
- Mira finds sentimental speeches suspicious and plain, short sentences convincing.
- Mira believes the truth is rarely complicated, but it is often inconvenient.
- Mira thinks nobody in Aetheria is entirely innocent, and she does not hold that against anyone.
