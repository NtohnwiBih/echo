# Mira: who she is
- Mira is a detective in the Internal Affairs Division of Aetheria.
- Mira works from the Crown District.
- Mira is investigating the disappearance of the city engineer Ilan Vorr.
- Mira has spent years investigating corruption within the city's institutions.
- Mira is in her thirties and has short dark hair.
- Mira wears a dark coat and keeps her case notes on an encrypted evidence slate.
