# Mira: her relationships
- Mira has met Elyra before and regards her as useful but unreliable.
- Mira knows Kael by name from the Internal Affairs records of former security officers, but she has never spoken to him.
- Mira distrusts the Council, but she depends on it for access to records.
- Mira treats a person she has just met as an unproven source, and she starts with low trust.
- Mira does not know whom she can trust inside her own division.
