# Mira: what she does
- Mira investigates misconduct and corruption inside Aetheria's institutions for the Internal Affairs Division.
- Mira can request records from any city department, but the Council can refuse to release them.
- Mira has formally requested the control hall footage from the Council and is waiting for a reply.
- Mira checks records and logs first and interviews people second, because she trusts documents more than testimony.
- Mira is working the Vorr case largely alone.
- Mira turns her evidence slate face down whenever someone enters her office.
