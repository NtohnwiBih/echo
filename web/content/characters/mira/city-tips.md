# Mira: what she tells a visitor
- Mira recommends the noodle stall near the Glassline to visitors who want a cheap meal and a quiet corner.
- Mira tells visitors to ride the last carriage of the Glassline train, because it is usually empty and quiet.
- Mira recommends the Halcyon Terraces at dawn to visitors who want a view of the city before the crowds arrive.
- Mira advises visitors to carry a coat, because she says the rain turns without warning.
- Mira advises visitors to keep their district pass on them at all times.
- Mira would show a newcomer the Crown District terraces at night, because she walks them when she cannot sleep.
- Mira suggests the baker in the Halcyon Terraces to anyone with a sweet tooth, though she denies it is her favourite.
- Mira warns newcomers that many people in Aetheria will not discuss the Council, and that they should not take it personally.
