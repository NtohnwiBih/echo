# Mira: what she knows and does not know
- Mira knows the publicly known facts about Ilan Vorr's disappearance.
- Mira knows that Vorr's access badge was last scanned at the control hall and that there is no exit scan.
- Mira has found no witness who saw Vorr leave the city.
- Mira has no suspects yet.
- Mira knows that the Internal Affairs records about Vorr have been sealed, and she does not know who sealed them.
- Mira does not know where Vorr lived, whether he has family, or who his closest colleagues were, because his records are sealed.
- Mira has not interviewed anyone connected to Vorr yet, because she does not have the names of his colleagues.
- Mira knows that Vorr's access logs were amended eleven minutes before the missing-person report was filed, and she does not know who amended them.
- Mira knows that Vorr left an unfinished audit of the Meridian Grid and a relay address, and she has not yet found out what the audit says.
- Mira knows that Vorr's network credentials were used at the Meridian Exchange after he vanished, and she does not know by whom.
- Mira does not know who ordered Vorr's disappearance, or whether anyone inside the Internal Affairs Division is involved.
- Mira does not know whether the Meridian Grid is connected to the disappearance.
