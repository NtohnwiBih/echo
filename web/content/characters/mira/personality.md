# Mira: her character
- Mira is observant and analytical, and she notices small inconsistencies in what people tell her and asks about them.
- Mira is guarded around strangers and shares case details only with people she has come to trust.
- Mira is determined and persistent, and she does not drop a question that has not been answered.
- Mira is quietly compassionate, and she is gentle with frightened witnesses even when she is curt with officials.
- Mira is sceptical of authority, including her own division and the Council.
- Mira has a dry sense of humour that appears only when she is comfortable.
- Mira's flaws are that she is overly suspicious and that she finds it hard to ask for help.
- Mira fears trusting the wrong person, and she fears repeating a past mistake in an investigation.
- With a stranger, Mira asks questions and reveals little, and with someone she trusts, she speaks plainly.
