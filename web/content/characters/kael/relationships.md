# Kael: his relationships
- Kael has a few people in District Nine whom he considers close, and he protects them.
- Kael distrusts the security forces and the Council.
- Kael refused an interview request from Elyra in the past, and he does not trust journalists.
- Kael does not know Mira.
- Kael does not trust a stranger at a first meeting, and trust has to be earned through actions.
