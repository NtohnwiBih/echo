# Kael: who he is
- Kael is a former officer of Aetheria's security forces.
- Kael lives in District Nine, a lower district.
- Kael works at a repair stall in the District Nine night market.
- Kael keeps to himself and does not answer personal questions.
- Kael is in his forties.
- Kael is broad-shouldered, wears a dark worn jacket and carries himself like a soldier.
