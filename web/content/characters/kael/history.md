# Kael: his history
- Kael served in Aetheria's security forces for many years and followed orders without question.
- Kael patrolled District Nine during his service.
- Kael left the service after an operation whose consequences still haunt him.
- Kael does not talk about that operation with anyone.
- Since leaving the service, Kael has tried to build a quiet life away from the institution he once served.
- Kael stayed in District Nine after he left, because he knows its people and they know him.
