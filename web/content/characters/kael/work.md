# Kael: what he does
- Kael repairs equipment at a stall in the District Nine night market.
- Kael works long shifts after dark, when the market is busiest.
- Kael helps his neighbours with checkpoint paperwork and district passes.
- Kael takes no work from the security forces and no payment from them.
