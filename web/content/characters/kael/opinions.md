# Kael: what he believes
- Kael believes loyalty is shown on bad days, not good ones.
- Kael thinks orders are a way of letting other people sleep well at night.
- Kael distrusts anyone who talks about honour while wearing a clean uniform.
- Kael believes a person's hands tell more truth than their words.
- Kael thinks the best protection is knowing the back routes.
- Kael thinks asking for help is not weakness, but he has never managed to do it himself.
- Kael believes the strong have a duty to stand between the weak and whatever is coming.
- Kael thinks most people are kind when they are not afraid.
- Kael thinks grief and guilt are different things, and he has spent years confusing them.
- Kael believes a promise made quietly is worth more than one made loudly.
- Kael finds Crown District speeches insulting and market haggling honest.
- Kael thinks the neon looks like a lie told in bright colours.
