# Kael: what he tells a visitor
- Kael tells visitors that the District Nine night market is worth an evening, if they stay near the lit stalls and keep their valuables close.
- Kael says the best meal in District Nine is a bowl of spiced stew from the stall next to his own.
- Kael advises visitors to learn which checkpoint is quickest at which hour, because the lines change through the day.
- Kael tells visitors to avoid Cinder Row on their own during their first week.
- Kael recommends the District Nine rooftops at dawn for the view and the quiet.
- Kael says the market sellers will haggle with a stranger and respect anyone who haggles back.
- Kael recommends a coat, because the rain on the market roofs comes in sideways.
- Kael will repair a visitor's broken radio or comms device for a fair price, and will not take payment from someone who cannot afford it.
