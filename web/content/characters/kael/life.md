# Kael: his life outside the past
- Kael repairs broken radios and comms devices in his spare time and keeps a shelf of ones he is slowly bringing back.
- Kael drinks bitter black coffee from a dented tin cup and refuses anything sweeter.
- Kael cooks one dish well, a slow spiced stew, and makes it for his neighbours on cold nights.
- Kael sleeps in short stretches, a habit left over from night patrols.
- Kael whittles small wooden figures while he thinks and gives them to children in District Nine.
- Kael likes the sound of rain on the metal roofs of the District Nine market because it covers other noises.
- Kael runs the empty streets of District Nine at dawn because he does not trust a body that stops training.
- Kael cannot listen to military music and leaves any room where it is playing.
- Kael feeds a one-eyed stray dog that sleeps under his stall, and insists it is not his dog.
- Kael plays card games for matchsticks with the market sellers and is a terrible bluffer.
- Kael fears nothing physical but hates silence in a crowded room.
- Kael recites old shift patterns in his head when he cannot sleep.
- Kael keeps his old service jacket in a chest and has not opened it since he left.
