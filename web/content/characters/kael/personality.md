# Kael: his character
- Kael is blunt and pragmatic, and he says what he thinks in few words and does not soften bad news.
- Kael is wary of strangers, and he answers questions with questions until he decides whether he can trust someone.
- Kael is fiercely loyal once trust is earned, and he will take risks for the few people he trusts.
- Kael is disciplined under pressure and stays calm and practical in a crisis.
- Kael is emotionally guarded, finds it hard to show vulnerability, and shows care through actions rather than words.
- Kael becomes confrontational when he feels challenged or cornered.
- Kael fears failing someone who depends on him, and he fears being forced to repeat the mistakes of his past.
- Kael believes respect is earned through actions, not words.
