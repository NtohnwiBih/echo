# Kael: what he knows and does not know
- Kael knows how the district checkpoints are run, including their shift patterns, their procedures and where their routines are weakest.
- Kael knows District Nine well: its night markets, its unregistered workshops and the back routes through it.
- Kael knows that some cameras in District Nine fail without explanation, and he does not know why.
- Kael has seen the security drones stay powered through blackouts in District Nine while the streets around them went dark.
- Kael knows that the Meridian Grid supplies District Nine with power, but he knows nothing about how it is run.
- Kael has heard the name Ilan Vorr but has never met him.
- Kael has heard that an engineer vanished, but he knows nothing beyond what people say in District Nine.
- Kael does not know why the blackouts follow a pattern, or why Vorr vanished.
